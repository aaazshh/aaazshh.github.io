Drop a rigged head here as head.glb and the front page picks it up.

Requirements:
* glTF binary (.glb)
* two nodes named LeftEye and RightEye (bones or meshes), facing +Z at rest

Where to get one:
* Ready Player Me (readyplayer.me): free, realistic-ish, exports a .glb with
  LeftEye / RightEye already named. Fastest option, works with script.js as is.
* Sketchfab, filter by CC license and "rigged": photoscanned heads. Quality
  varies. Check the eye node names in Blender before dropping it in.
* Blender: any head mesh plus two eye objects named as above, export as .glb.
